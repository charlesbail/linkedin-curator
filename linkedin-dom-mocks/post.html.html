Widget Week continues with UNDO! Our old pal Command-Z.

The AI Widgets group has been researching widgets to add AI intelligence to. All this week, we'll be posting our research on specific interaction techniques.

"People often undo several times in a row to get back to a version they remember, rather than a specific number of steps. AI could recognize that pattern and make a suggestion where they’re trying to land. Even better, a user could semantically describe a point at which they want to return and let the system find it, while also providing information about what other changes this may affect."
